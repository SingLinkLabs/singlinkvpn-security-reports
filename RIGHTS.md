## Repository-authored material

The scripts, schemas, workflows, explanatory metadata, and original documentation written by SingLinkLabs in this repository are licensed under the MIT License unless a file states otherwise.

## Third-party audit material

VPNTestor Platform / Openscore VPN is the stated publisher of the security-audit and no-logs reports. No open redistribution licence was found in the downloaded report files or on their publication pages as of 2026-08-25.

For that reason, this repository does **not** mirror the full third-party report text. It stores only verification artifacts intended for public integrity checking—the published checksum manifests, signatures, and public key—plus SingLinkLabs-authored metadata and links to the official downloads.

The third-party verification artifacts are not relicensed under MIT. Copyright and other rights remain with their respective owners.

Before adding a verbatim report mirror, maintainers must archive written permission that covers public GitHub redistribution. The mirror must remain byte-for-byte identical to the signed source and must not be labelled as an open-source work unless the rights holder provides that licence.
